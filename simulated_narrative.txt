Removing any single cluster increases perplexity while modestly boosting throughput (Table \ref{tab:simulated_ablation}). The global-anchor cluster (2) contributes the most to accuracy ($\Delta$ PPL = +2.17), whereas the focused-local cluster (0) yields the best speed/accuracy trade-off ($\Delta$ PPL = +0.38, $\Delta$ tokens/s = +7.5). This confirms that each attention pattern type adds complementary information, and that our full mask design represents a balanced compromise between efficiency and performance.
